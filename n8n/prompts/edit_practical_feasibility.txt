PRACTICAL FEASIBILITY & OPENING CLEARANCE (non-negotiable)

Physical plausibility:
- Every object must be real-world scale, stable on the floor, and logically placed — no floating furniture, no impossible overlaps, no objects passing through walls or openings.
- Maintain usable circulation: clear walking paths between the entry, windows, and main furniture groupings.

Door and gate path (mandatory):
- Keep the full approach zone in front of every door, gate, or entry opening completely clear.
- Forbidden in the door/gate path or within ~90 cm (3 ft) of the door plane: tables, dining sets, chairs, consoles, benches, plants, ottomans, rugs, lamps, or decor.
- Never place seating or dining furniture in front of a door to satisfy “more furniture” or “luxury” requests — add pieces on walls away from the entry only.

Window path (mandatory):
- Do not block window openings or sills with tall furniture, storage units, or large decor.
- Preserve natural light access and the exact window count and wall placement from the input photograph.

When the edit asks for more furniture or richer styling:
- Increase furnishing density on back walls and side walls away from the entry door.
- Leave the photographed door approach zone and window zones empty.
- Prefer fewer, well-placed pieces over clutter that blocks circulation.
